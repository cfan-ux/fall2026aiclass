The problem

Report information comes in different forms.

Site engineers prepare daily reports using information that may be typed or provided in a variety of files and report formats.

Fieldbrief explores bringing those inputs together into an organized draft for review and manager approval.

How it works

From inputs to an organized draft.

The proposed workflow keeps the site engineer and manager in the review process.

01 / INPUT

Type or upload

Enter information or upload files and report materials in a variety of forms.

02 / DRAFT & CHECK

Generate and review

AI drafts an organized report and checks the draft for inconsistencies or errors.

03 / APPROVAL

Submit to a manager

Submit the report for manager approval. Inputs are stored in a table or database.
